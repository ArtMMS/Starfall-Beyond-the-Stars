**Starfall: Beyond the Stars** is a space exploration mod for Minecraft. Build your own modular spaceship, fly it with a friend, survive hostile environments, and witness rare cosmic events, both among the stars and back home in the Overworld.

> ⚠️ **Early development.** Most features below are planned and not yet implemented.

---

## ✨ Planned Features

### 🚀 Modular Spaceships
- Build ships piece by piece: **engines, fuel tanks, batteries, solar panels, onboard computers, seats and habitable modules**.
- Extract and collect **fuel** on planets and in space.
- Every module has its own **health** and can be destroyed. Ship status is shown on the **pilot panel** and the **central panel**.
- Reach space and **orbit planets**.

### 👨‍🚀 Pilot & Co-Pilot
- Up to **two players** can operate different ship systems.
- **Pilot:** steering and acceleration.
- **Co-pilot:** navigation, power management, radar and auxiliary systems.

### 🛋️ Ship Interior
- Walk through the ship's corridors while it is **parked**.
- Before departure, everyone must be **seated**. The ship only launches when all conditions are met.
- Switch between first and third person independently from the piloting system.

### 🤖 Onboard AI
- A voiced ship computer with **failure alerts, planet information, fuel consumption** and **hazard warnings**.
- Optional **voice commands** through [Simple Voice Chat](https://modrinth.com/plugin/simple-voice-chat).

### ☄️ Space Events
- **Meteor Clouds** *(rare)*: slow down and dodge, or take damage based on your speed.
- **Alien Raiders** *(rare)*: hostile, technologically advanced aliens trying to board and loot your ship.
- **Lost Civilization Ship**: a drifting derelict carrying ruins and monuments from a forgotten world.

### 🌍 Overworld Events
- **Falling Meteor** *(extremely rare)*: impacts of varying size and destructive power.
- **Alien Abduction** *(rare, night or thunderstorms only)*: a peaceful alien ship descends and abducts a mob with a green tractor beam.
- **Crashing Ship** *(ultra rare)*: a burning alien vessel crashes somewhere in the world. Loot its technology and find records written in a **forgotten language** that can be deciphered for recipes, coordinates and lore.

### 🧑‍🚀 Suits & Survival
- **Space suit, helmet, mask, oxygen tank and filters**.
- Oxygen, pressure and environmental protection systems.
- Optional dedicated slots through **Curios** and **Accessories**.

### 📡 Detection & Communication
- **Atmosphere Panel**: a block that monitors the world's atmosphere and announces events by voice.
- **Space Radio**: send messages into space. You may pick up strange signals, interference... and, very rarely, receive an answer days later.

### 🪐 Planets
- Each planet will have its own biomes, structures, creatures, resources, energy and fuel sources, and environmental conditions: giant waves, glass rain, eternal darkness and more.
- Planets are discovered gradually. *(Details coming soon.)*

### Optional Integrations

| Mod | Purpose |
|---|---|
| [Curios API](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/curios) | Dedicated slots for oxygen tanks and filters |
| [Accessories](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/accessories) | Alternative accessory slots |
| [Simple Voice Chat](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/simple-voice-chat) | Voice commands for the onboard AI |

Starfall works fully without any of these.

## 🛠️ Building from Source

```bash
git clone https://github.com/ArtMMS/Starfall-Beyond-the-Stars.git
cd Starfall-Beyond-the-Stars

# Run the game client
./gradlew runClient

# Build the mod jar (output in build/libs/)
./gradlew build
```

On Windows, use `gradlew.bat` instead of `./gradlew`.
